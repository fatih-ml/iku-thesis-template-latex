This study presents a bilingual (Turkish and English) LaTeX template for the
graduate theses of Istanbul Kultur University, fully compliant with the
official thesis writing guide.
The template automatically applies every formatting rule -- page margins, font,
line spacing, heading formatting, page numbering, and the ordering of front and
back matter -- so that students can focus on content rather than formatting.
References follow the MLA style, and a single class flag switches the entire
document between Turkish and English.
The template is designed to compile without modification on both Overleaf and
local TeX distributions.
As a result, it reduces student workload and improves the conformance of theses
to institutional standards.
